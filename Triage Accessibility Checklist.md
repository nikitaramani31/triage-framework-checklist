# The triage *checklist*

A short, practical accessibility checklist sorted the way teams fix things: what design decides, what content says, and what code builds. Each item links to the WCAG 2.2 rule behind it.

- **A** · the floor: without it, people are blocked
- **AA** · the standard and legal target
- **AAA** · the stretch, nice where you can

Tools: your keyboard, browser zoom, [WAVE](https://wave.webaim.org/), [WebAIM Contrast Checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/), [WCAG 2.2 quick reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/).

1. **Scan**List what’s on the page.
2. **Shortlist**Turn off what isn’t there.
3. **Quick pass**WAVE, contrast, one Tab run.
4. **Go deep**Mark each item, rank your top 3.
